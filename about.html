About SOS

Named for the children we couldn't save, working for the ones we can.

Save Our Souls Foundation was established in 2007 for the common good of underprivileged children. We work in communities across the USA and other parts of the world to nurture and improve the quality of life of children in need.

The foundation is named in honour of children who are dying from preventable diseases, and every programme we run starts from that promise.

Frequently asked

Where does SOS work?

In communities across the USA, with a focus on inner-city America, and in Africa.

What does the foundation focus on?

Preventing child deaths from diseases like malaria, measles, polio, tuberculosis, waterborne illness and HIV/AIDS, through health care, clean water, sanitation, education and sustainable livelihoods.

How can I help?

You can donate, volunteer your time or skills, or host a fundraiser. Visit our Get Involved page to start.
