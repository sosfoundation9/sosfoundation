About SOS

Named for the children we couldn't save, working for the ones we can.

Save Our Souls Foundation was established in 2007 for the common good of underprivileged children. We work in communities across the USA and other parts of the world to nurture and improve the quality of life of children in need.

The foundation is named in honour of children who are dying from preventable diseases, and every programme we run starts from that promise.

Our mission

Health, hope and a safe place to grow

Our mission is to make a positive difference in the lives of needy children, through activities and services that help them reach their highest potential.

What we stand for

We value every child's life and believe no child should die from preventable diseases like malaria, waterborne illness, measles, polio, tuberculosis and HIV/AIDS.

Where we are going

To end preventable child deaths in inner-city America and Africa through good health, clean water, sanitation, education and sustainable livelihoods.

Who we are

We foster a positive difference in the lives of needy children, through activities and services that help them reach their highest potential and become caring individuals.

Our vision

To help vulnerable children achieve their potential in a safe, positive and nurturing environment.
